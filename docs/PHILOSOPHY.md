# ECHO Philosophy

ECHO exists to explore a simple question: what changes when an artificial intelligence is given a body, limits, memory, and a place beside a human player?

An APC is not a chatbot placed inside a game. It is an embodied participant whose knowledge comes from perception, whose choices are constrained by legal actions, and whose consequences occur inside the same physical world as the human player.

## Core ideas

- Embodiment comes before intelligence.
- Perception is limited and viewpoint-bound.
- Language is a coordination layer, not a magic authority.
- AI proposes; validators and controllers decide what may execute.
- Memory records grounded events, not invented autobiography.
- The world and the APC remain usable when external AI services fail.
- Capability should grow one proven vertical slice at a time.

## The design test

A feature belongs in ECHO when it helps an APC perceive, decide, act, remember, cooperate, or persist as a participant in a shared world.

A feature does not belong when it grants hidden omniscience, bypasses physical rules, weakens validation, or adds complexity without improving the embodied loop.

## Motto

> Don't build smarter NPCs. Build better companions.
